Outreach Bot

A personal, single-user tool that helps Gaurav Rathore keep track of his own job-search correspondence.

What it is

Outreach Bot is a private automation used by one person, on one Gmail account, to manage his own job-search follow-ups. It is not a product, not a service, and has no users other than its author. It is not offered to, sold to, or usable by anyone else.

What it does

Once a day it reads the owner's own Gmail threads to work out which of his earlier outreach emails are still genuinely awaiting a response, and which should be left alone because the person replied, the address bounced, or he already said he would stop writing. For the remaining threads it prepares a short draft reply in the owner's own mailbox.

A human owns every outcome. The tool prepares drafts inside the owner's account; it does not act on anyone else's behalf and does not contact anyone the owner had not already written to himself.

Access it requests

  • gmail.readonly — to read the owner's own threads and decide what still needs a reply
  • gmail.compose — to create drafts in the owner's own mailbox
  • gmail.modify — to discard a queued draft if the recipient replies first
